Roadside checklist — do this before you get stuck

What to keep in your car before winter: a 10-item roadside checklist

  1. Jump starter (or jumper cables)
  2. Tire inflator
  3. Tire plug kit
  4. Headlamp or flashlight
  5. Spare fuse assortment
  6. Mylar emergency blankets, one per seat
  7. Wool blanket
  8. Recovery tow strap
  9. Water and non-perishable snacks
  10. Phone charging cable or power bank

What to look for

  • Jump starter peak amps: about 1000A covers most 4-cylinder and V6 gas engines. V8s, trucks and diesels need 2000A or more, especially in cold weather.
  • Jumper cable gauge: lower numbers are thicker. 8-gauge or lower (6 or 4) carries more current and works better on big engines.
  • Tire inflator PSI: pick one that lets you set a target pressure and shuts off when it gets there. Use the pressure on your driver's door sticker, not the number on the tire.
  • Power source: plug-in inflators run off the 12V socket and never need charging. Cordless ones are handier but need to stay charged.
  • Blankets: mylar is small and cheap. Wool is bulkier but stays warm when damp and lasts for years.

FAQ

What should be in a car emergency kit?

At minimum: a jump starter or jumper cables, a tire inflator, a tire plug kit, a headlamp, spare fuses, emergency blankets, water, snacks, a phone charger and a tow strap. The checklist above covers all ten.

Do I need a jump starter or are jumper cables enough?

Jumper cables need a second car. A portable jump starter doesn't, which matters at night, in a parking garage or somewhere remote. Keep it charged, because a dead jump pack is no help. That is the same lesson as the jump-start how-to video.

What does an OBD2 scanner do?

An OBD2 scanner (or code reader) plugs into the port under the dash on 1996 and newer cars and shows the code behind the check engine light. A basic reader clears codes; a better scan tool can show live data. Reading it yourself before a shop visit is the point of the OBD2 scanner tutorial.

Do mylar blankets actually work?

They reflect body heat back to you and block wind, which helps when the engine is off. They tear easily and get noisy, so many people keep a wool blanket too.

Can I drive on a plugged tire?

A plug in the tread is meant to get you to a shop. Have the tire checked and patched properly. Don't plug the sidewall or a large cut.

When should I switch to winter tires?

When overnight temperatures stay near freezing, all-season rubber hardens and loses grip. Dedicated winter tires stay softer in the cold. For pickups and trucks, match load range and size to the door sticker, then mount them before the first hard freeze—not after the first ice storm.
